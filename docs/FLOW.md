# SoldOut: the flow (agreed spec)

## 0. Onboarding
Organizer gives:
- **Goal**: seats, deadline, price, date, place, audience, enrollment/payment link
- **Content**: answer pack, FAQ, Drive videos, brand files
- **Network**: contacts for warm intros (past families, mentors, partners): name, relationship, community, contact
- **Accounts to act from**: organizer's LinkedIn, Instagram, email (outreach is mostly sent as the organizer)

## 1. Strategy (Good Strategy / Bad Strategy kernel)
- **Diagnosis**: what is actually in the way of filling the seats
- **Guiding policies** (2–3), each with a cheap **test** and success signal
- Each policy points to **target communities / organizations**

## 2. Community research → GO / NO-GO
For each target community or org: who they are, what they need, why the program fits (or doesn't),
how to get in. SoldOut recommends **go / no-go** with its reasoning; the organizer can override.

## 3. People in the community
- Find people we can reach in each GO community, **connectors first** (biggest networks, most trusted)
- **Person research**: role, why they matter, public channels (LinkedIn, Instagram, email, WhatsApp number)
- Public, professional information only; no bought lists, nothing about anyone's children

## 4. Person card (organizer approves)
Who · why them · community · best channel · opening message (as the organizer) · content to share later

## 5. Channel ladder (one step at a time, 2–3 days between steps)
1. **LinkedIn**: connect with a short note → message once accepted
2. **Email**: personal email
3. **Instagram** DM
4. **Warm intro** (last resort): a ready-to-forward message for someone in the organizer's network

WhatsApp is used once the person replies or messages JOIN (no cold WhatsApp: platform bans and TCPA risk).
Every first message says who is writing and why; one follow-up at most; "no thanks" is permanent.

## 6. Connector conversation (not a broadcast)
1. **Open**: regular, personal message about them and their community. No pitch, no links.
2. **Listen**: respond, ask about their community's needs.
3. **Interest** → share progressively: one line on the program → a short video → the PDF if they want details.
4. **Ask**, based on where they are:
   - interested for their own family → fit questions → link → paid
   - not buying → "who do you know who'd love this?" / "is there a group, newsletter or event I could share it in?"
5. **Hand-off**: something easy to forward + their own **JOIN code** (attribution)
6. **Record**: bought / referred N / shared to group X / not now → **next connector**

Voice: openers **as the organizer**; follow-ups mostly as the organizer, assistant voice where natural.
**Human in the loop**: anything odd, sensitive or uncertain is flagged; the organizer can **take over any
conversation at any time** (AI pauses until handed back).

## 7. Parents (built)
JOIN code → fit questions → answers only from content → right format (text / video / PDF) →
refunds, installments, unknowns → organizer → enrollment link → paid.

## 8. Learn
Scoreboard per community, policy, connector and channel: opt-ins, seats, referrals.
Pace vs goal. The strategy is redrafted from what converts.
